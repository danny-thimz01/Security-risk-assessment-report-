# Security Risk Assessment Report

A short write-up of the security hardening steps I'd recommend to reduce risk and keep our systems safer.

## What's in here

I picked three hardening methods that give the most protection for the least fuss:

### 1. Firewall maintenance
A firewall is only useful if it's kept up to date. This means regularly checking and updating its rules and settings so we stay ahead of new threats instead of reacting after something goes wrong.

### 2. Configuration checks
Routine checks help us spot unauthorized changes to our systems early, whether they're accidental or someone messing where they shouldn't be.

### 3. Multifactor authentication (MFA)
MFA asks users to prove who they are in two or more ways before getting into a system or network. Even if a password gets stolen, an attacker still can't walk in.

## Why these three?

Together they cover the basics: keep the perimeter strong (firewall), watch for anything unexpected (config checks), and make sure only the right people get in (MFA).
